Questions

How much does it cost?

Nothing while we launch. You can send up to 10 letters a day.

Does she need to sign up?

No. She opens your link and types the 4-digit code you give her.

What if she presses “No”?

Kaptar carries the button away. On the third try he flies off with it, and a little hand presses “Yes”.

Can you read my letter?

No. Your phone locks the letter with her code and a secret part of the link before it is sent, and that part never reaches us.

How long does the link work?

120 days after you send it. Once she answers, it stays for 150 days after her answer, then it is deleted.

Why sign in with Google?

So your letters and her answers are saved to your account, and only you can see them.
